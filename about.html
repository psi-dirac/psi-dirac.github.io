The Planet–Star Interaction (PSI) Group sits within the DiRAC Institute in the Department of Astronomy at the University of Washington. We study the material left over from planet formation by watching how it changes the light of its host star.

Most of what we look at is faint, slow, or irregular, which means the work is as much about survey methodology as it is about individual objects. A typical project runs from a survey light curve, through archival infrared photometry, to a spectroscopic follow-up campaign, and the group is organised so that a student can see all three stages.
